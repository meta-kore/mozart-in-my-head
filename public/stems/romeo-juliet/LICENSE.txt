They are adapted from The Spheres Dataset, a recording of P. I. Tchaikovsky's
Romeo and Juliet, Fantasy Overture, performed by the Colibrì Ensemble:
https://zenodo.org/records/17347681

Changes: the 33 instrument lines folded to mono and summed into seven
families, the passage from 9:54 to 12:48 of the recording (the love theme)
cut as a loop with a 2.5-second crossfade at the seam, one common gain
applied, and encoded as Ogg Opus.
