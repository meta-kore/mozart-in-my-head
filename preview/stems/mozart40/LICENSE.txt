They are adapted from The Spheres Dataset, a recording of W. A. Mozart's
Symphony No. 40 in G minor, K. 550, performed by the Colibrì Ensemble:
https://zenodo.org/records/17347681

Changes: instrument lines folded to mono and summed into seven sections, the
opening 123.55 seconds (first-movement exposition) cut as a seamless loop, one
common gain applied, and encoded as Ogg Opus.
