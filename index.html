Mozart in My Head

Pull an orchestra apart with your hands: Mozart's Symphony No. 40, or Tchaikovsky's Romeo and Juliet. Best on a Meta Quest: press Enter XR, sit back, and use your hands. On a computer: click the ball to start, click again to open it up, then drag the sections around, scroll to move them nearer, and click one to hear it alone. The cards under the ball switch the music; Space (or the button between them) pauses.
